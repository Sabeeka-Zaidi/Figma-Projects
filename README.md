## 📌 Project Overview

Jewel Collective is an online marketplace that allows independent jewellery designers to showcase and sell their unique, handcrafted jewellery and accessories.

The original website had an outdated visual design and created several difficulties for customers. Buyers could not purchase products directly through the platform and instead had to contact individual designers by email and use third-party services to complete their purchases.

The website also lacked effective search and filtering functionality, making it difficult for customers to discover products.

This project focuses on redesigning Jewel Collective into a modern, elegant, and user-friendly e-commerce platform that makes jewellery discovery and purchasing simple and enjoyable.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this redesign is to create a modern e-commerce experience that:

- Makes it easy for customers to discover unique jewellery.
- Allows customers to purchase products directly from the website.
- Provides intuitive search and filtering functionality.
- Highlights independent jewellery designers and their stories.
- Creates a sophisticated visual identity for the brand.
- Improves navigation and overall usability.
- Builds customer trust through product information, reviews, and transparent policies.
- Provides a seamless experience across different devices.

### Core Objective

> **To create a luxurious, user-friendly e-commerce platform that connects customers with independent jewellery designers while making the discovery and purchasing experience seamless.**

# 🔎 Problem Statement

The original Jewel Collective website had several usability and design issues.

### Problems Identified

**1. Outdated Visual Design**

The visual design did not effectively communicate the premium and artistic nature of the jewellery.

**2. Complicated Purchasing Process**

Customers could not purchase products directly through the website.

Instead, they had to:

`Find Product → Contact Designer → Email → Third-Party Service → Purchase`

This created unnecessary friction in the shopping journey.

**3. No Effective Product Search**

Customers could not easily search for specific jewellery products.

**4. Limited Filtering**

There were insufficient filtering options to help customers narrow products based on:

- Category
- Price
- Designer
- Material
- Style
- Collection

**5. Limited Product Discovery**

The website did not provide an efficient way for customers to discover new designers and collections.

**6. Weak Brand Experience**

The previous design did not strongly communicate the craftsmanship, elegance, uniqueness, and premium nature of the brand.
